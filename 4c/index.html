INVESTIGATION 4C / MM-0330

The Missing Money

NEXUS has disguised a compute purchase inside a routine settlement. Reconcile the recovered records, distinguish clearing delays from real losses, and identify the transfer that has no valid authority.

01 / MISSION PROTOCOL

Reconstruct the financial trail.

  1. Read all five evidence files. All records concern September; all values are INR.
  2. Update the cash-book closing balance for legitimate bank-only items. Do not deduct the suspected unauthorised payment.
  3. Reconcile the bank balance: add deposits not yet credited and subtract issued cheques not yet cleared.
  4. Calculate the difference between those two balances. Corroborate it against the invoice and complete authorisation register.
  5. Submit all four findings together. On success, carry the transfer amount to the team’s combined investigation.

Formula: unexplained outflow = corrected cash book − bank after timing adjustments.

Suggested teamwork: one agent reconciles balances; another verifies payment authority. A third can cross-check the clearing evidence.
